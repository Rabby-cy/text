# 鼠族派系扩展：金鸢尾兰

“我曾经做过一个梦。”
“在那场梦里，我去了一个盛开着金色的鸢尾兰的地方。那里的人们温柔而友善，社区的氛围和谐而繁荣。没有战乱，没有纷争。我不知道那个地方叫什么名字，也许，它叫乌托邦。”

"I once had a dream. In that dream, I went to a place where golden irises bloomed. The people there were gentle and kind, the atmosphere of the community was harmonious and prosperous. There were no wars, no strife. I do not know the name of that place, perhaps, it is called Utopia."

## 简介：

本模组在鼠族本体的基础上，新增了一个来自银河系外的星际鼠族帝国：金鸢尾兰帝国。该帝国推崇和谐，氛围友善，若玩家有意与他们交善，那么他们也会尽力帮助玩家，反之也不会畏惧玩家的威胁。

本模组内容：

-  1个新的鼠族派系，并且拥有特殊的袭击方式与声望系统。
-  2种新的作物，25种新的枪械，9把新的近战武器，28件配件与头饰，34件服装与外套，20作用各不相同的建筑，27项鼠族科技。
-  2个新的文化模因与相关戒律，3个新的基因。
-  230个金鸢尾兰专属背景故事以及2位金鸢尾兰故事叙述者。

本模组为鼠族本体的扩展，需要鼠族本体作为前置，同时必须拥有皇权。兼容文化，生物科技与CE（不完全）。
本模组英文翻译为机翻（因为制作人不懂英语），因此英语质量不会太高，希望有人能够制作翻译补丁。

This module builds upon the Ratkin base mod, introducing an interstellar Ratkin empire from outside the Milky Way: the Oberonia Aurea Empire. This empire values harmony and has a friendly atmosphere. If players wish to establish good relations with them, the empire will make an effort to assist the players. Conversely, they will not shy away from a player's threats.

Contents of this module include:

-  1 new Ratkin faction with unique raid tactics and a reputation system.
-  2 new crops, 25 new firearms, 9 new melee weapons, 28 accessories and headgear, 34 pieces of apparel and coats, and 20 buildings each with distinct functions.
-  27 Ratkin technologies.
-  2 new cultural memes and associated doctrines, and 3 new gene.
-  230 Oberonia Aurea-specific background stories and 2 Oberonia Aurea story narrators.

This module is an expansion of the Ratkin base and requires the Ratkin base mod as a prerequisite, as well as the Royalty expansion. It is compatible with Cultures, Biotech, and Combat Extended (CE)(incomplete).

The English translation of this mod is machine-translated (as the creator does not speak English), so the quality of the English may not be high. Hopefully, someone can create a translation patch to improve it

The anticipated Oberonia Aurea expansion is expected to be divided into four parts, which are, in the following order:
